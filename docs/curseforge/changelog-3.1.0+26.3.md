## Beach Craft (Raft) 3.1.0 for Minecraft 26.3

Build your own raft and sail it, meet the people who stayed on the Endless Sea, and listen: the sea has its own sounds and music now. Days and nights come and go out at sea, new flowers grow on the islands, and many places fit better into the world.

### Build your own raft
- **Foundations on the water**: look at open water and place a Raft Foundation (or a Reinforced Foundation). It settles into the sea with its top level with the waves. Set more against its sides and your raft grows block by block.
- **Build on it**: floors, walls, a roof, chests, furnaces, crafting tables, crops and redstone. Everything that stands on the foundations sails along. Keep it inside the edge of the foundations.
- **Helm**: set it on your raft facing the bow and use it to **set sail**. W and S sail, A and D steer. Sneak to leave the helm: the raft slows down and waits where it is. Use the wheel while you steer (or sneak and use the helm) to **drop anchor**: the raft turns to the nearest compass direction and becomes blocks again, with every chest, furnace and crop just as it was.
- **Everything works at sea**: open chests and furnaces, craft, farm, flip levers, climb ladders, place and break blocks (in survival it takes as long as on land, and the drops land on deck). Furnaces keep smelting and crops keep growing while you sail, and the raft's own lanterns light it up at night.
- **Power**: the **Oar** (rowing, on the deck edge), the **Sail** (wind; stack sails for a taller mast, every sail counts) and the **Raft Motor** (the most power; use furnace fuel on it, even while you sail). Without them you can only paddle slowly. Heavy rafts need more power: they are slower and turn more slowly.
- Friends, animals and dropped items ride along on deck. Sailing rafts bump into each other, into land, ice and piers.
- To set sail a raft may have up to 1024 blocks and be up to 32 blocks across. At sea it can still grow a little: set Raft Foundations against its side.
- Recipes: **Helm** (Ship Wheel on top of a stick on top of 3 planks), **Sail** (rope, 2 Sail Canvas and 2 sticks), **Oar** (Paddle, rope and a stick) and **Raft Motor** (scrap metal, a circuit board, a furnace and a titanium ingot).
- New advancement **Shipwright**, and a new Almanac page "Your Own Raft" in the Sailing chapter.
- The Expedition Raft stays as it is: a ready-made ship for those who do not want to build.

### People of the Sea
- **Eight people** live in the old places of the Endless Sea: **Keeper Tobias Fenn** at the Old Lighthouse, **Nalu** on the veranda of Nana Kalea's house in Saltmere, **Old Tamo** on the docks of Saltmere, **Dr. Imara Voss** in the Ashen Forge, **Teo** behind the counter of the tiki bars, **Kai** at the fire of the tent camps, **Dr. Elin Lund** in the iceberg outposts and **Patience Okafor** in the control room of the rusted rigs.
- **Right-click to talk.** Ask them about their place, what you should do next (a hint for your current step in the story), and for rumours about other places and people. What they say changes as your story goes on.
- **Welcome gifts** the first time you meet: Fenn's old spyglass, a Potion of Water Breathing from Nalu, cooked cod from Tamo, a Piña Colada from Teo, a warm drink from Dr. Lund and a circuit board from Okafor. Dr. Voss hands you a Potion of Fire Resistance for the way to the Ember Sigil.
- **Trading**: seven of them trade. The coin of the sea is pirate gold and pearls, many of them also buy fish and finds, and Kai trades his flotsam for your cookies. Trades restock every 10 minutes.
- They go about their day: Fenn watches the sea through his spyglass, Tamo fishes from the pier, Kai sits at the camp fire, the others work at their barrels, grills, terminals and control panels.
- They never leave their homes, nothing out here can hurt them, and the story never depends on them: every story item still waits in its chest.
- Places from older worlds get their person too, the first time you come near.
- New advancements **A Friendly Face** and **Every Soul Aboard** (meet all eight), a "People of the Sea" section with a page for each of them in the Almanac, and spawn eggs in the Creatures tab.

### Sounds and music
- **Every creature has its own voice**: sharks thrash and gnaw at your raft, seagulls laugh when they steal your fish, whales groan, jellyfish crackle when they sting, and the anglerfish's lure hums in the dark. The Leviathan roars, splashes, slams and dives with sounds made for it.
- **Machines sound like what they are**: water boiling in the purifier, food sizzling on the grill, the wind turbine's rotor, the hand crank of the air compressor, the boom of the ship cannon, the air burst of the harpoon gun, the motor of the sea scooter and the reel of the plastic hook. Hammocks creak.
- **Story moments**: the Tidewarden Key being forged, the Tide Seal opening and the Leviathan answering from the abyss.
- **Sea ambience**: waves lapping at your raft and gulls far away. In the dark deep you may hear things you would rather not.
- **Music**: a calm sea theme by day and another one at night, own themes for Port Solace, Saltmere, Helix Station, the Old Lighthouse, the Abyssal Trench and the Tidewarden temples, and a battle theme while the Leviathan's boss bar is shown. At sea the music comes back more often than in the overworld.
- All sound effects are made for Beach Craft. The music is by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), licensed under CC BY 4.0; the credits are on the mod page and in CREDITS_MUSIC.txt in the jar.

### World
- **Day and night**: the Endless Sea now has the same day/night cycle as the overworld, with sunrise, sunset, moon and stars. Bring lights for the night. Solar panels only work by day.
- **Structures blend in**: island places (castaway camps, beach shacks, the Old Lighthouse, the Ashen Forge) only appear on real land, so no more square blocks of sand and gravel standing in the sea. No more box-shaped terrain cuts around big places.
- **Coral Temple**: always rests on a reef floor deep enough to keep the whole temple under water.
- **Iceberg Outpost**: the hut now stands on a real iceberg with a jagged peak and a deep keel; the roof, lantern and antenna no longer float.
- **No floating blocks**: fixed loose blocks above sunken ruins and around the camps; the upturned lifeboat of the castaway camp rests on its props.
- **Sea Oats**: the upper and lower half of the plant now fit together.
- **Hibiscus and Bird of Paradise** now grow in clumps on most palm and jungle isles.

### New plants
- **Plumeria**, **Beach Morning Glory**, **Tropical Orchid**, **Spider Lily**, **Heliconia** (two blocks tall) and **Aloe Vera** grow on the islands.
- All of them make dye, and Plumeria, Tropical Orchid and Aloe Vera fit into flower pots.

### Items
- **Sea Scooter**: three times the charge, almost four minutes of thrust.

### Notes
- Needs **Minecraft 26.3**, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer and **Fabric API**. Team Reborn Energy is included.
- **Existing worlds** work: the new plants and the better structure placement show up in newly explored chunks, and the people move into the places you have already found.
- Rafts you build only sail on water. Beds and signs on a sailing raft wait until you drop anchor.
