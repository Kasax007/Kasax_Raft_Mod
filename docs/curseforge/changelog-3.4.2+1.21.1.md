## Beach Craft (Raft) 3.4.2 for Minecraft 1.21.1

**This is the Minecraft 1.21.1 version of Beach Craft 3.4.2** (Fabric Loader 0.16+, Fabric API 0.116.x for 1.21.1), with the same changes as 3.4.2 for Minecraft 26.3: machines keep their energy, a fairer Chunk Destroyer, an Item Catcher with room for its catch, a Plastic Hook that no longer pulls through walls, and no more driftwood saplings.

- **Machines keep their energy**: break a Makeshift Battery, Makeshift Solar Panel, Quarry, Chunk Destroyer, Wind Turbine or Air Compressor and its item keeps the energy that was in it. The tooltip shows how much ("Energy: 120,000 / 1,000,000 FE"), and the machine has it back when you place it again. Items with different amounts of energy do not stack, empty ones stack with new ones as before.
- **Chunk Destroyer**: only a column in which it really mines something costs 100,000 FE. Columns with nothing to mine (only air, water, lava or bedrock) are free and passed over at once, even when it has no energy left, so empty parts of a chunk no longer hold it up.
- **Item Catcher**: nine slots for the catch instead of one, so it no longer stops catching when a second kind of debris comes along. Hoppers take the catch out of all nine slots and still only put nets into the net slot. The catcher shows the newest kind of catch on top. An Item Catcher from an older world keeps its catch in the first of its nine slots.
- **Plastic Hook**: it only pulls items with nothing solid in the way. No more pulling items through walls, floors or glass.
- **Driftwood is no tree**: the Driftwood Sapling no longer comes from drifting barrels or driftwood leaves (the leaves drop sticks now) and is gone from the creative tabs. Saplings already in your world stay as they are.
- The Seafarer's Almanac and the tooltips tell all of this.

Worlds from 3.4 for 1.21.1 load as they are.
