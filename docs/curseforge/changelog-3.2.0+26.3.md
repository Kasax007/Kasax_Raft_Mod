## Beach Craft (Raft) 3.2.0 for Minecraft 26.3

Better sailing for the rafts you build yourself (a real sail with a mast, an anchor, a fuel screen for the motor, a wheel that turns), a wider and calmer Endless Sea (more open water, fewer and better placed structures with better loot, seagulls in the sky, islands full of animals), a real challenge on the way to the Leviathan (guarded structures, new enemies and mini-bosses, a harder final fight), and a clean-up of block models: no more ragged edges, open sides or flickering faces.

New worlds get the new sea. In an existing world only the parts you have not explored yet change, so where old and new parts meet an island can end in a straight edge.

### Your own raft
- **Sail and Mast**: the new **Mast** is a pole you can stack as high as you like. **Sails** are now panels of canvas: set them side by side and on top of each other and they join into one big sheet, with the yard along the top and a rope hem along its edges, no seams between the blocks. Next to a mast the canvas reaches over to it. Every block of sail adds wind power, so a bigger sail sails faster (up to a limit for heavy rafts). Use a sail (with an empty hand or anything that isn't a block) to **furl** it: the canvas rolls up under the yard and the raft no longer catches the wind. Use it again to set it.
- **Anchor Winch**: a new anchor winch for the deck edge, anchor over the side. Use it to drop the anchor: a sailing raft stops and holds its position, even when the helmsman pushes on. Use it again to raise it and sail on. On land it is a winch with its anchor up or down.
- **Raft Motor fuel screen**: click the motor (on land or at sea) to open its fuel bay, like the Expedition Raft's engine: put coal, charcoal, logs or other furnace fuel in, or take it out again. The screen shows the flame and how long the fuel lasts. The motor burns one item at a time while you sail forward; everything in it stays when the raft turns back into blocks.
- **The wheel turns**: at the helm, the ship's wheel turns left and right as you steer and comes back when you let go. Everyone on board sees it.
- **Mooring**: turning the raft back into blocks (use the wheel while you steer, or sneak and use the helm) is now called mooring, since the raft has a real anchor now. Sneak and use the wheel while you sit at the helm moors the raft too.
- **Setting sail without lag**: the first time a raft set sail in a world, the game generated a whole stretch of sea far away for the raft's blocks, which made it stall for seconds. That place is now generated empty, so setting sail is quick every time.
- Recipes: **Mast** (3 logs in a column, makes 4), **Anchor Winch** (rope, 2 planks and 2 iron ingots).
- Sails from 3.1 keep their canvas; their mast is now a block of its own (set a Mast next to them).

### The Endless Sea
- **More open sea**: islands are about half as common as before and mostly palm beach islands with sand and lagoons; jungle islands are a rarer variant now. Palm isles grow more palms.
- **Fewer structures, none inside each other**: every kind of structure is spread out further (about 60% fewer in total), rusted rigs keep far apart from each other (no more three rigs in one spot), and two Beach Craft structures never grow into each other.
- **Better places**: the Old Lighthouse stands only on low, fairly flat coast with the sea close by (no more terraces cut into wooded hills), Saltmere only over open shallow water (its stilts no longer cut into island slopes), and the Helix Station research lab only on a deep sea floor, with at least 10 blocks of water above it. It is also rarer.
- **Old Tamo** walks to the edge of the dock and fishes into the water in front of him (he stood inland and cast at the planks).
- **Richer chests**: every structure chest has more in it: Beach Craft materials, raft parts, food and seeds that fit the place, and now and then a highlight such as an Expedition Raft upgrade, diving gear, a cutlass or a harpoon.
- **Flotsam** is a small bonus now: a single item now and then instead of a stream (about a tenth of before, at most five pieces around you, rarer barrels).
- **Item Catcher**: catches only while its raft sails (a raft you built and set sail with): flotsam it sails through, and now and then a catch out of the sea, more the faster you sail. Standing still or with the anchor down it catches nothing. The Expedition Raft fishes flotsam it sails through into its storage too.
- **Seagulls** fly in over the sea in small flocks every few minutes when you are out on the water, also near islands full of gulls.

### Danger on the way: guarded structures
Exploring was a scavenger hunt without a fight until the Leviathan. Now most structures are guarded, and the guards get tougher the further the story leads you:
- **When you come close** to a guarded structure for the first time, a warning sounds (a shout, a horn, the hum of the reef...) with a line on screen, and two seconds later its guards appear around the unopened chests. Once per structure; they stay until you beat them, and they never stand in the way of the story.
- **Start** (abandoned raft, beach shack, fishing boat wreck, Old Lighthouse, iceberg outpost): a few scavengers with fists, wooden blades or a bow, drowned, strays.
- **Middle** (sunken plane, sunken ruins, cargo ship wreck, Helix Station, rusted rig): scavengers with stone and iron blades and leather armour, reef guardians, drowned groups, and the **Scavenger Chief** on the rig.
- **Late** (Port Solace, Ashen Forge, Coral Temple, and Saltjaw's pirate cove and galleon): corsair crews, blazes and ember crabs in the forge, the **Corsair Captain**, the Scavenger Chief of Port Solace and the **Reef Warden** of the Coral Temple.
- Easy has one guard less per group, Hard one more; nothing in Peaceful. Saltmere, the castaway camp and the sanctum stay quiet.

### New enemies
- **Scavenger**: a sunburnt looter with a bandana, brass goggles and a backpack. Fights with what he carries (fists, blades or a bow, keeping his distance like a skeleton), calls his gang when hit. Drops scrap, plastic, rope, coins.
- **Reef Guardian**: a guardian overgrown with coral. Charges its beam for a few seconds before it hits (watch the glow) and its spikes hurt. Drops prismarine, sea glass and pearls.
- **Corsair Captain** (mini-boss, boss bar): bigger than his corsairs, armoured, with a cutlass. At half health he blows his horn and two corsairs climb out of the deep to help him. Always drops his cutlass and a purse of gold, often a harpoon.
- **Scavenger Chief** and **Reef Warden** (mini-bosses, boss bars): the elite of a scavenger gang (iron armour and sword) and a huge reef guardian. Their drops: gold, scrap and often a harpoon gun; pearls, sea glass, prismarine and now and then Ancient Energy.

### Islands full of life
- Turtles on the beaches, parrots in the palms, (golden) rabbits in the dunes, chickens and pigs on the bigger green islands, reef fish in the lagoons, foxes and white rabbits on the frost floes, more seagulls and crabs on the volcanic isles.
- New islands start with their animals: far more creatures are placed when an island is generated.

### The Leviathan
- **The Tidewarden Key is spent** when you call the Leviathan at the altar (not in creative mode). The altar does not take it while the Leviathan is awake or when its pool is not there; a new fight needs a new key.
- **It keeps to the deep**: while it circles it stays well under the surface and away from the walls, so it can only be hit when it rises on purpose (to spit, to roar, to lunge), each with its warning.
- **A harder fight**: 750 health instead of 600, armour 12 and toughness 2 instead of 10, bite 10 instead of 6, slam 13 instead of 8, brine bolts 7 (9 in the last phase) instead of 5, roar 4 and 7 instead of 2 and 4. Phase 2 starts at 60 % (was 50 %), phase 3 at 30 % (was 25 %), it is faster in both and chains more attacks at the end.
- **Tidelings** bite for 6 instead of 3 and have 14 health instead of 10; a roar calls 4 of them (6 in the last phase, up to 7 at once).
- **Rewards**: more Leviathan Scales (12-18), 800 experience, and everything lands on dry floor next to you instead of in the pool.

### Cleaner edges
- **Satellite Dish**: the dish is round now. It was a square plate with a round picture on it, so its edges showed a square frame and torn white bits around the dish. The rim has its own clean colour, and the dish, its back and the base plate lost their blotchy noise.
- **Control Panel**: the sides under the sloped desk are closed (you could look into an open wedge), the desk no longer reaches into the screen housing, and the panel's metal is clean instead of blotchy.
- **Ship Wheel** and the wheel of the **Helm**: built pixel by pixel, so the rim and spokes have proper sides. Before, a square frame and ragged strips stood around the wheel.
- **Shark Jaw Trophy**: the jaw has sides along its teeth, the stray strip under it is gone and the teeth are no longer doubled.
- **Rusty Pipe Valve**: the red hand wheel is round, without a square frame.
- **Wanted Poster**: the torn corner no longer leaves a floating edge.
- **Seashells**: each shell's sides follow its shape (they were boxes with see-through corners).

### No more flickering (z-fighting)
- Blocks: faces that lay exactly on top of each other flickered when you moved. They are separated now on the **Neon Tubes**, the **Figurehead**, the **Deck Chair**, the **Beach Umbrellas**, the **Air Compressor**, the **Ship Cannon**, the **Oar**, the **Cushions**, the **Leviathan Trophy**, the **Chart Table** (its map lay almost on the table top) and the **String Lights**.
- Creatures and the Expedition Raft: where two parts of a model touched in the same plane (fins on bodies, bands, the raft's anchor gear, oars and lanterns, crab legs, shark jaws, ...) the smaller one now always lies on top.
- Structures: a few decorations stood inside each other: the chart table under a steel pillar in the Helix Station, a lightning rod on top of a buoy lamp, bottles hovering on a chair's back in the galleon wreck, a deck chair leaning into a railing in Saltmere, and the steel rods of rebar concrete poking through glass panes, shelves and lanterns in Port Solace.

### Textures
- **Raft Motor**: the shaft and clamp are plain steel instead of smeared stripes.
- **Jolly Roger** item: sharp pixels (it was a blurred, shrunk copy of the flag).
- **Unrefined Ocean Garbage** and **Ancient Broken Ring**: hard edges instead of half see-through pixels.

### Other
- **New mod icon**: the beach chunk from the CurseForge logo.

Worlds from 3.1 load as they are.
