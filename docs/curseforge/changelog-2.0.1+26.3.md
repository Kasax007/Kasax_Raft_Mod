## Beach Craft (Raft) 2.0.1 for Minecraft 26.3

A bugfix release. Every feature was filmed for a review, and everything that did not work as it should was fixed.

### Fixed
- **Bruce the shark now spawns.** In the always-bright raft ocean the monster light check rejected every natural spawn. Sharks also no longer despawn after a few seconds in daylight, and they stop hunting you once you climb back onto your raft.
- **Sail Canvas can be crafted again**: it shared the 2x2 rope recipe with the Rope Coil. New recipe: 2 rope on top of 2 white wool gives 2 sail canvas (the red-striped canvas and the raft flag follow).
- **Scarecrow**: its head now faces the same way as its body. Jockey riders of blocked spawns are blocked too.
- **Item Catcher**: no more ghost item on top after emptying it; the net slot only takes nets and the catch slot is take-only (also for hoppers).
- **Water Purifier**: hoppers below can take out the empty bucket; the progress arrow no longer shows a sliver when idle.
- **Simple Grill**: right-clicking it with a non-food item (or a third food) no longer takes food off the grill.
- **Piña Colada** gives the bowl back and sounds like a drink.
- **Fresh Water** fills one drumstick (it gave 2.5 and claimed to quench a thirst that does not exist).
- **Coconuts** grow back on palm leaves after about 20 minutes (it took about 95 hours).
- **Driftwood and palm wood** (logs, planks, stairs, slabs, fences, doors...) burn in furnaces.
- **Quarry and Chunk Destroyer** no longer delete the contents of shulker boxes they mine.
- **Batteries and cables** no longer push the same energy back and forth; a cable network gets its full rate from a battery. Solar panels and batteries send far fewer updates to clients.
- **Tooltips**: furnace stats, cable rate and battery capacity show real numbers instead of '%d'.
- **Raft sky**: the moon is no longer drawn on top of the sun; undead burn in the endless daylight.
- **Raft Portal**: the frame drops with any tool; a broken raft-side portal can be relit with the Energy Staff; the overworld exit portal looks for a free spot nearby instead of cutting into builds and trees.
- **Raft Teleporter**: lands within 5 blocks in every direction, never in a cave under lava, without a 1-block drop, and other players see the stone block it places.
- **Music discs**: one disc per shipwreck chest (not up to five), and the jukebox stops when the song ends.
- **Titanium ore** drops experience; titanium blocks resist explosions like iron blocks; the full armor set effects end a few seconds after a piece comes off.
- **Metal Detector** and armor texts are translatable; the detector answers in the action bar.
- **Shark Jaw Trophy** sits flush on the wall.
- **Wood**: driftwood and palm stairs, slabs, fences and gates catch fire like oak; leaves and saplings go into the composter, saplings burn a little.
- **Quarry and Chunk Destroyer** save their progress, and their finished pit stays dry (kelp and seagrass no longer leak water into it). Right-click shows the stored energy and the mining position.
