Beach Craft brings the open-sea survival of the game Raft into Minecraft.
Build a driftwood portal, step through, and you stand on a tiny platform above an endless ocean.
Debris drifts past, a shark circles below, and every plank you fish out of the water makes your raft a little bigger.
Minecraft 26.3, Fabric, needs Fabric API.

  • The Raft dimension: one huge ocean in endless daylight, with sandy palm islands, sunken shipwrecks, floating driftwood and titanium deep under the sea floor.
  • Ocean debris: the current carries planks, palm leaves, plastic and scrap metal past you from the west, and now and then some lost loot.
  • Plastic Hook: hold right-click to pull floating items towards you.
  • Item Catcher: put it in the water and it catches the debris that floats into it. With a net inside, it also fishes up ocean garbage.

What drifts past and what the catcher finds:

  • Every 10 seconds, 1 or 2 pieces of debris appear 20 to 40 blocks west of you and float east with the current. Up to 16 pieces drift around each player.
  • Most debris is driftwood planks, palm leaves, plastic or scrap metal.
  • About 6% of the debris is lost loot: coconuts, cooked cod, glass bottles, string, rope, iron nuggets, coal, palm and driftwood saplings, titanium nuggets or a circuit board.
  • The Plastic Hook reaches 12 blocks in the direction you look.
  • The Item Catcher holds one kind of item at a time, so empty it often. Hoppers can take the catch out.
  • With an Item Catcher Net inside, a catcher in the raft ocean fishes up Unrefined Ocean Garbage about every 30 seconds. A net lasts 32 catches.
  • Smelt the garbage into a Trash Cube and right-click it for 1 to 3 random items: fish, kelp, sticks, nuggets, plastic, slime balls, a bucket, and with some luck a diamond or an emerald.

 

The way to the sea starts in the overworld.
Find some ancient treasure, build a driftwood portal and light it.

  1. Find Ancient Broken Rings in shipwreck treasure, buried treasure and ocean ruins, or fish them up. Smelt them into Ancient Energy.
  2. Shipwreck treasure also holds Ancient Crystals. Ancient Energy, an Ancient Crystal and a stick make the Energy Staff.
  3. Short, dark driftwood stumps without leaves stand on sandy beaches. Four driftwood logs around one Ancient Energy make 4 Portal Frames.
  4. Build the frame like a nether portal and light it with the staff. You arrive on a driftwood platform high above the waves, with a portal back home.

Once you have titanium, the Raft Dimension Teleporter takes you to the sea and back without a portal.

Recipes and where to find the ancient items:

  • Ancient Energy: smelt or blast an Ancient Broken Ring.
  • Energy Staff: Ancient Energy on top, Ancient Crystal in the middle, stick at the bottom. 64 uses.
  • Driftwood Portal Frame (gives 4): Ancient Energy in the center, one driftwood log on each side.
  • Raft Dimension Teleporter: 5 titanium ingots and 2 Ancient Energy (ingot on top, energy - ingot - energy in the middle, 3 ingots at the bottom). 64 uses. Use it in the raft dimension to go back to the overworld.
  • Ancient Broken Ring: shipwreck treasure (60%, 1-2), buried treasure (80%, 1-3), ocean ruins (30%) and fishing treasure (25%).
  • Ancient Crystal: shipwreck treasure (50%, 1-5).
  • The frame needs an empty space of at least 2x3 inside. A broken return portal on the raft side can be lit again with the staff.
  • Driftwood stumps grow on overworld beaches. A driftwood sapling from the ocean loot grows a new stump on sand.

 

Out at sea you need a floor under your feet, clean water and a warm meal.
Almost everything here is made from what the ocean brings you.

  • Raft Foundations: planks bound with rope. Reinforced Foundations add scrap metal.
  • Rope: from palm leaves or string. Climbable Rope Ladders and Rope Coils for decoration.
  • Water Purifier: turns water bottles into Fresh Water. A water bucket gives three.
  • Simple Grill: cooks two foods at once, 50% faster than a campfire.
  • Crop Plot: farmland that is always watered and never trampled. Crops grow about 50% faster on it.
  • Scarecrow: no hostile mob spawns within 24 blocks, no trampled farmland within 8.
  • Coconuts: right-click palm leaves that carry a coconut to pick it. The leaves grow a new one after about 20 minutes.
  • Drinks: Fresh Water, Coconut Milk (Regeneration) and the Piña Colada (Speed for 2:30).

How the survival blocks work:

  • Water Purifier: water bottle or water bucket in the top slot, fuel (planks, logs, coal or charcoal) in the fuel slot. A bottle takes 10 seconds, a bucket 20 seconds. Hoppers work.
  • Simple Grill: right-click it with fuel (planks, logs, coal or charcoal), then with raw food. It holds two foods and drops them when they are cooked. Right-click with an empty hand to take the last food back.
  • Crop Plot: never dries out and never turns back into dirt. On every random tick the crop on it has a 50% chance of an extra growth step.
  • Scarecrow: the trample protection works for normal farmland too.
  • Fresh Water: fills one drumstick and gives the bottle back.
  • Coconut: fills 3 drumsticks.
  • Coconut Milk: Regeneration I for 5 seconds.
  • Piña Colada: fills 4.5 drumsticks and gives Speed for 2:30, a short Regeneration and a moment of Nausea. You get the bowl back.

Survival recipes:

  • Rope: 2 palm leaves on top of each other, or 3 string in a column for 2 rope.
  • Rope Coil: 4 rope (2x2). It crafts back into 4 rope.
  • Rope Ladder (gives 6): rope - stick - rope in all three rows.
  • Raft Foundation (gives 4): 7 planks, with rope in the top middle and the bottom middle.
  • Reinforced Foundation: a Raft Foundation and 2 scrap metal.
  • Water Purifier: 5 plastic around a glass bottle, 3 scrap metal at the bottom.
  • Simple Grill: 3 scrap metal on top, 2 sticks as legs below.
  • Crop Plot (gives 3): 3 dirt on top of 3 planks.
  • Scarecrow: coconut on top, stick - palm thatch - stick in the middle, stick at the bottom.
  • Coconut Milk: a coconut and a glass bottle.
  • Piña Colada: sugar, coconut and bowl in a column.
  • Plastic Hook: 2 plastic on top, plastic and rope below, a stick at the bottom right.
  • Item Catcher: 8 driftwood logs in a ring. Item Catcher Net: 7 string in an H shape.

 

Bruce is back. The shark hunts anyone who swims in the raft ocean.
Climb back onto your raft and you are safe again.

  • Beat him for Raw Shark Meat and Shark Teeth.
  • Hang his jaw on your wall as a Shark Jaw Trophy.

Shark facts:

  • Bruce spawns now and then in the upper water of the raft ocean, never on Peaceful.
  • He has 13 hearts and only goes after players in the water, unless you start the fight.
  • He drops 1-3 Raw Shark Meat and 0-2 Shark Teeth.
  • Raw shark meat can give you Hunger. Cooked shark meat (furnace, smoker, campfire or Simple Grill) fills 4 drumsticks.
  • Shark Jaw Trophy: 3 planks on top of 3 shark teeth.
  • The spawn egg is in the creative tab.

 

The sea is big.
These tools help you find your way and the treasure below the waves.

  • Receiver: right-click to see the direction and distance to the nearest floating driftwood.
  • Paddle: right-click in a boat for a push forward.
  • Metal Detector: right-click the ground and it tells you at which height the first titanium ore below is.
  • Titanium: ore deep under the sea floor (diamond pickaxe). A full set of titanium armor gives Water Breathing, Dolphin's Grace and Conduit Power.
  • Music: three ocean music discs hide in shipwreck treasure.

Explore details and recipes:

  • Circuit Board: copper ingot - slime ball - copper ingot on top of 3 plastic.
  • Receiver: shows the compass direction, the degrees and the distance in blocks. Recipe: titanium nugget on top, scrap metal - circuit board - scrap metal in the middle, 3 scrap metal at the bottom.
  • Paddle: one push every 1.5 seconds. Recipe: 4 planks (2x2, top right) and a stick at the bottom left.
  • Metal Detector: checks the column straight below the block you click. Recipe: titanium ingot - circuit board - titanium ingot on top, an ingot in the middle, 3 ingots at the bottom.
  • Titanium Ore and Deepslate Titanium Ore only generate in the raft ocean, below Y 20. Smelt or blast the raw titanium. 9 nuggets make an ingot, 9 ingots make a block.
  • Titanium Armor: the usual armor shapes with titanium ingots, repaired with titanium ingots. The set effects end a few seconds after you take a piece off.
  • Music Discs: Some Game Music, Lofi Beat and Crystal Castles by Seth Makes Sounds (CC0). Each one has a 10% chance in a shipwreck treasure chest.

 

The sea has its own small tech line.
It uses the Team Reborn Energy API, so machines from mods like Tech Reborn can connect.

  • Makeshift Solar Panel: 10 FE per tick. At sea the sun always shines.
  • Makeshift Battery: stores 1,000,000 FE.
  • Copper and Titanium Cables connect it all.
  • Quarry: mines an 11x11 hole down to the bottom of the world, then drains it and turns any water or lava around the pit into glass.
  • Chunk Destroyer: mines the whole chunk it stands in.
  • Brick and Titanium Furnaces: a faster Brick Furnace (1.5x) and a very fast Titanium Furnace (32x).

Machine numbers:

  • Makeshift Solar Panel: 10 FE per tick when it sees the sky. In the overworld only by day, in the raft dimension always. Stores 100,000 FE. Right-click shows the stored energy.
  • Makeshift Battery: 1,000,000 FE, up to 10,000 FE per tick in and out.
  • Copper Cable: 128 FE per tick. Titanium Cable: 512 FE per tick.
  • Quarry: 1,000 FE per block. Put a chest on top and the drops go into it. It saves its progress, and a right-click shows its energy and mining position.
  • Chunk Destroyer: 100,000 FE per column. A chest on top collects the drops, and it also drains its pit and turns any water or lava around it into glass.
  • Brick Furnace: 1.5x speed, 1.5x fuel use.
  • Titanium Furnace: 32x speed, 16x fuel use, and every result is doubled.

Machine recipes:

  • Makeshift Solar Panel: 3 circuit boards on top, 3 iron ingots in the middle, iron - redstone - iron at the bottom.
  • Makeshift Battery: iron - circuit board - iron, iron - redstone - iron, iron - circuit board - iron.
  • Copper Cable (gives 6): 3 copper ingots on top of 3 redstone. Titanium Cable (gives 6): 3 titanium ingots on top of 3 redstone.
  • Quarry: iron blocks in the corners, circuit boards on the sides, a titanium ingot in the center and a diamond pickaxe at the bottom middle.
  • Chunk Destroyer: titanium blocks in the corners, circuit boards on the sides, a Quarry in the center and a diamond pickaxe at the bottom middle.
  • Brick Furnace: 7 bricks around a furnace, Ancient Energy at the bottom middle.
  • Titanium Furnace: 8 titanium blocks around a Brick Furnace.

 

Driftwood and palm wood, palm thatch, sail canvas, a ship wheel, the raft flag and coconut lanterns:
everything you need for a raft that looks like home.

All building blocks and their recipes:

  • Driftwood and palm wood: log, wood, stripped log and wood, planks, stairs, slab, fence, fence gate, door, trapdoor, button, pressure plate and sapling. Palm wood also has leaves. Both woods burn in furnaces and catch fire like oak. Leaves and saplings go into the composter.
  • Palm Thatch (gives 3): 4 palm leaves (2x2). Also as stairs and slab.
  • Sail Canvas (gives 2): 2 rope on top of 2 white wool. Add red dye for Red Striped Sail Canvas.
  • Raft Flag (gives 2): red striped sail canvas on top of 2 sticks.
  • Ship Wheel: sticks in the corners, planks on the sides, scrap metal in the middle.
  • Coconut Lantern: rope, coconut and torch in a column.
  • Plastic Block and Scrap Metal Block: 9 plastic or 9 scrap metal. They craft back into 9.
  • Titanium Grates: 5 titanium ingots in an X.
  • Titanium Block and Raw Titanium Block: 9 ingots or 9 raw titanium.

 

New in 2.0.1

A bugfix release. Every feature was tested in the game, and everything that did not work as it should was fixed.
Bruce now spawns, Sail Canvas can be crafted again and the Paddle really pushes your boat.

All fixes in 2.0.1:

  • Bruce the shark now spawns naturally in the bright raft ocean. He no longer despawns after a few seconds in daylight and stops hunting you once you climb back onto your raft.
  • Sail Canvas can be crafted again. New recipe: 2 rope on top of 2 white wool gives 2 sail canvas.
  • Paddle: the push now really moves the boat.
  • Scarecrow: its head faces the same way as its body. Riders of blocked spawns are blocked too.
  • Item Catcher: no more ghost item on top after emptying it. The net slot only takes nets, the catch slot is take-only (also for hoppers).
  • Water Purifier: hoppers below can take out the empty bucket. The progress arrow stays empty when idle.
  • Simple Grill: right-clicking it with a non-food item (or a third food) no longer takes food off the grill.
  • Piña Colada gives the bowl back and sounds like a drink.
  • Fresh Water fills one drumstick.
  • Coconuts grow back on palm leaves after about 20 minutes (before: about 95 hours).
  • Driftwood and palm wood burn in furnaces. Their stairs, slabs, fences and gates catch fire like oak. Leaves and saplings go into the composter.
  • Quarry and Chunk Destroyer no longer delete the contents of shulker boxes they mine. They save their progress, and their finished pit stays dry. Right-click shows the stored energy and the mining position.
  • Batteries and cables no longer push the same energy back and forth. A cable network gets its full rate from a battery. Solar panels and batteries send far fewer updates to clients.
  • Tooltips: furnace stats, cable rate and battery capacity show real numbers.
  • Raft sky: the moon is no longer drawn on top of the sun. Undead burn in the endless daylight.
  • Raft Portal: the frame drops with any tool. A broken portal on the raft side can be lit again with the Energy Staff. The overworld exit portal looks for a free spot nearby instead of cutting into builds and trees.
  • Raft Dimension Teleporter: lands within 5 blocks in every direction, never in a cave under lava and without a 1-block drop. Other players see the stone block it places.
  • Music discs: a shipwreck chest holds one copy of a disc instead of up to five, and the jukebox stops when the song ends.
  • Titanium: the ore drops experience, titanium blocks resist explosions like iron blocks, and the armor set effects end a few seconds after a piece comes off.
  • Metal Detector and armor texts can be translated. The detector answers in the action bar.
  • Shark Jaw Trophy sits flush on the wall.
