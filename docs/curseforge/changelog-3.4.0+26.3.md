## Beach Craft (Raft) 3.4.0 for Minecraft 26.3

A feedback update: a cooking pot you can watch, a fishing rod with its own tackle box, JEI and Lootr support, sorted
creative tabs, rarer and better-placed big structures with fuller barrels, and many fixed textures and models.

### The Endless Sea: more room, fuller barrels, Lootr

- **Big places are rarer and further apart**: oil rigs, research stations, galleon and cargo ship wrecks, sunken planes, pirate coves, iceberg outposts, the drowned city of Port Solace, the Coral Temple and the Abyssal Sanctum now spawn much less often, and two of these big places never start closer than 384 blocks to each other, whatever kind they are (no more rig right next to a wreck next to a temple). Rusted rigs keep at least 800 blocks apart (was 600). Small things (fishing boats, sunken ruins, rafts, buoys, driftwood) are as common as before. Measured over 540 km² of sea: about 1.8 big places per square kilometre instead of 6.8, and the nearest big place is usually about 500 blocks away instead of 190.
- **The story still leads you everywhere**: every story place still generates, and the Expedition Journal's charts, the story chests' maps and /locate only ever point to places that really are there. The lighthouse, Saltmere and the Ashen Forge keep their old frequency; the sea places of the story are a bit further apart.
- **These distances apply to newly explored parts of the sea.** Places you have already found stay where they are.
- **Barrels and crates with loot**: structures were full of barrels and crates that were all empty. Now a good share of them hold a few useful things that fit the place: rope, planks, plastic and scrap, food, fish bait, seeds of the island crops (pineapple crowns, chili seeds, rice, sweet potatoes, banana shoots), gold coins, ship's stores on wrecks and at the pirate cove, city goods in Port Solace. The salvage barrels of the rig, the Helix Station and Port Solace sometimes hide a titanium ingot, an oxygen tank or a golden lure. The real chests stay the best loot of a place.
- **Lootr support**: with Lootr installed, every structure chest and barrel becomes a Lootr container, and Beach Craft's own wooden crates and treasure chests work the same way while keeping their look: every player gets their own loot. Without Lootr nothing changes. Lootr is optional, Beach Craft does not need it.
- **Structures stand on the ground**: big places such as the Ashen Forge, the Abyssal Sanctum, the Coral Temple and the Helix Station now settle onto the real ground under their whole footprint and are no longer built where part of them would hang high above a slope (the Ashen Forge vault sometimes stood 30 blocks above its island's shore). Where a structure still stands a little higher than the ground, its bottom now goes down to the ground in the rock or sand that is there, with a rough slope beside it, and the stilts of Saltmere and the legs of the rig reach the sea floor. No more buildings hanging in the water.

### Cooking Pot
- **You can see it cook**: the ingredients bob in the pot and are slowly stirred round, the water turns the colour of the dish while it cooks, bubbles and steam get busier as it gets done, and a chime rings when the dish is ready. A finished pot holds the stew, and the dish turns above it until the last bowl is served.
- **No more wrong ingredients**: the pot only takes an ingredient that still fits a dish together with what is already in it. Anything else stays in your hand, and the action bar says why.
- **It tells you what is missing**: after every ingredient the action bar says what the pot can still become ("For Island Curry add Sweet Potato and Coconut"), or that the dish is ready to cook but needs a fire below.
- **Changed your mind?** Sneak-use the pot with an empty hand and you get every ingredient back. The tooltip, the Almanac and the action bar now say so. A pot from an older world that holds a mix that makes nothing turns murky, smokes and tells you how to empty it.

### Driftwood Fishing Rod
- A new rod from 3 driftwood planks and 2 string, with **its own tackle box**: one slot for Fish Bait and one for Golden Lures.
- **Loading it**: sneak-use the rod to open the tackle box, or right-click bait and lures onto the rod in your inventory (right-click it with an empty hand to take them out). The tooltip shows what is loaded.
- **Every cast uses one of each that is loaded**: bait makes fish bite 5 seconds sooner (like Lure I), the Golden Lure brings better treasure (like Luck of the Sea II) and faster bites too, on top of the rod's own enchantments. It only uses what is loaded, never bait from your bag.
- Lasts twice as long as a plain rod (128 uses), takes all fishing rod enchantments and is repaired with driftwood planks.
- Plain fishing rods work as before: bait or a lure in your off hand or bag is used one per cast.
- New **Fishing** page in the Seafarer's Almanac's Gear & Gadgets chapter.

### JEI support
- With **Just Enough Items** installed (optional, Beach Craft does not need it) you get pages for the **Cooking Pot** (ingredients in any order, the dish and its bowls, the heat sources and the cooking time), the **Fish Trap** (everything it catches and how often, with or without bait) and the **Island Crops** (what to plant where, sea grapes under water and the wild plants of the islands), plus info pages for Fish Bait, the Golden Lure, the Driftwood Fishing Rod, the fishing rod, the Cooking Pot, the Fish Trap and the Crop Plot.

### Creative tabs
- The ten Beach Craft tabs now sit together in a sensible order: **Raft & Survival**, Building, Decoration, Cozy and Nature in the upper row, Island Kitchen, Gear, Voyage, Creatures and Tides of the Deep in the lower one.
- Every tab is sorted into groups: wood sets (log, planks, stairs, slab, fence, gate, door, trapdoor, button, plate) and each stone with its stairs, slabs and walls together, glass with its panes, lights, furniture and pirate decor apart, food from wild plant to seed to finished dish, weapons, armour sets, tools and fishing gear together, creatures with their drops, and the story items in the order you find them.
- The palm and driftwood wood sets moved to Building, the palm and driftwood leaves and saplings to Nature, coconuts and coconut drinks to the Island Kitchen, the shark drops to Creatures, and the shell tile and pumice brick blocks to Building.

### Better looks
- **Banana**: the banana now looks like a banana: a yellow curved fruit with a brown stalk and tip. The banana plant has broad leaves, and a ripe plant carries a real bunch of bananas with the purple flower below it.
- **Island kitchen food**: every dish has new art in the vanilla style. The soups and stews sit in the same bowl as vanilla stews, with the food clearly on top: Island Curry with rice, Seafood Chowder with shrimp, Sweet Potato Soup with cream, a whole crab in the Chili Crab, a heap of fruit in the Fruit Salad and two scoops of Coconut Ice Cream. The Sushi Roll, Banana Bread, Tropical Smoothie, Baked Sweet Potato, Cooked Rice, Sweet Potato and Chili Pepper were redrawn too.
- **Rope Rug**: the corners around the round rug no longer show a light blue hole. You now see the floor under it.
- **Hurricane Lantern**: a new top with a dark iron cap, a chimney and a wire handle over it, standing or hanging. The brass is smooth now.
- **Coconut Planter, Driftwood Flower Box and Coral Vase**: you no longer see through them. The planter is half a coconut with soil and a white rim, and the flower box is filled with soil. The vase is closed, with a glass lip round its opening.
- **Potted Monstera and open Wicker Basket**: the soil and the lining show inside, so you no longer see through them.
- **Thatch Parasol**: it is now three blocks tall, so you can stand under it. The canopy is a little wider. All parts still break together and drop one parasol, and it still counts as shade for comfort. Parasols you placed before stay as they were.

### Rafts in the sea
- **No more flowing water next to raft foundations.** A foundation set into the sea now keeps the sea's water. When you break one, or a shark bites one off, the gap fills with still water instead of a patch of flowing water. Rafts that drop anchor do the same. Older foundations get it when you break them, or the next time their raft sails and drops anchor.

### Compatibility
- Optional, not needed: **Just Enough Items** (recipe pages) and **Lootr** (per-player loot).
- Worlds from 3.3 load as they are. The new distances, the footings under structures and the barrel loot apply to newly
  explored parts of the sea.
