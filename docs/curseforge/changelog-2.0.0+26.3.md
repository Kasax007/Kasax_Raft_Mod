## Beach Craft (Raft) 2.0.0 for Minecraft 26.3

**Ported to Minecraft 26.3 (Fabric, Java 25).** Needs Fabric API.

### New: survive the sea
- **Raft Foundation** and **Reinforced Foundation** - planks bound with rope
- **Rope** (from palm leaves or string), climbable **Rope Ladders**, **Rope Coils**
- **Water Purifier** - water bottles become **Fresh Water** (a bucket gives three)
- **Simple Grill** - cooks two foods at once, faster than a campfire
- **Crop Plot** - always watered, never trampled, crops grow faster
- **Scarecrow** - no hostile spawns within 24 blocks, no trampled crops within 8
- **Coconut Milk** (Regeneration), **Plastic Hook** (hold right-click to pull in debris), **Paddle** (push your boat), **Receiver** (direction and distance to the nearest island)
- **Ocean debris** - planks, palm leaves, plastic, scrap metal and loot barrels drift through the raft ocean

### New: Bruce the shark
- **Shark** in the raft ocean - attacks swimmers, drops **Shark Meat** and **Shark Teeth**
- **Shark Jaw Trophy** for your wall, shark spawn egg

### New: decoration
- **Palm Thatch** (block, stairs, slab), **Sail Canvas** (plain and red-striped), **Ship Wheel**, **Raft Flag**, **Coconut Lantern**, **Scrap Metal Block**, **Plastic Block**

### Changes
- Own portal (no Custom Portal API any more): driftwood portal frame, lit with the Energy Staff
- Driftwood trees now also grow on overworld beaches, so the portal can be built before you reach the sea
- One craft gives **4 portal frames**
- **Ancient Broken Rings** can be found in shipwreck treasure, buried treasure and ocean ruins (and by fishing)
- The raft dimension is taller, the arrival platform sits high above the waves
- The Plastic Hook no longer overshoots, crop plots give a stronger growth boost
