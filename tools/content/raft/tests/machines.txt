# Beach Craft 3.5 machines: every machine with an energy storage keeps its energy when it is broken (Makeshift
# Battery, Makeshift Solar Panel, Quarry, Chunk Destroyer, Wind Turbine, Air Compressor). The drop carries it in the
# raft:stored_energy component, the tooltip shows it, and placed again the machine has it back. One drop per machine
# and nothing more (no copy), batteries with different energy do not stack, with the same energy they do, an empty one
# stacks with a new one. Then the Chunk Destroyer in the void of the End: a column with blocks costs 100,000 FE, empty
# columns (air, bedrock) cost nothing and do not hold it up, also without energy.
